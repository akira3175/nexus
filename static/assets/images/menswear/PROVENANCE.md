# Menswear image provenance

All PNG files in this folder were generated for the Nexus redesign with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-22.

- `hero-tailoring.png`: original wide editorial photograph of an adult Vietnamese male model in a charcoal tailored suit, subject on the right and warm-gray negative space on the left; no text, logo or third-party branding. The supplied Figma frame was used only as a composition and mood reference.
- `navy-suit.png`: original square catalogue photograph of a navy single-breasted suit on a warm cream studio background.
- `ivory-shirt.png`: original square catalogue photograph of an ivory cotton dress shirt on a warm cream studio background.
- `charcoal-trousers.png`: original square catalogue photograph of charcoal pleated trousers on a warm cream studio background.
- `tobacco-blazer.png`: original square catalogue photograph of a tobacco linen blazer on a warm cream studio background.

Shared constraints: photorealistic menswear catalogue finish, realistic fabric texture, no text, logo, watermark, model identity reuse or visible third-party branding.
